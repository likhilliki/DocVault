# DocVault — 3-Minute Demo Script

## Setup

1. Open the frontend URL
2. Sign in: `demo@docvault.app` / `DocVault2026!`
3. 10 pre-loaded documents — laptop, iPhone, insurance, degree, PAN, Aadhaar, internship offer, bank statement

---

## Scene 1 — Dashboard (30s)

Show the dashboard. Point out:
- **10 documents**, **4 receipts**, **3 active warranties**
- Attention Required section showing expired return deadlines
- Warranty days remaining calculated in real-time (661 days on the laptop)

> *"Everything uploaded is organized automatically. Warranties, expiries, all tracked."*

---

## Scene 2 — Upload a Receipt (40s)

1. Click **Documents → Upload**
2. Drop any receipt image (JPG or PNG)
3. Watch: `PENDING → PROCESSING → READY`
4. Click the new document — see extracted fields: merchant, amount, warranty, return deadline

> *"I uploaded a receipt. DocVault identified it as a RECEIPT, extracted the merchant, amount, and calculated the warranty expiry. Zero manual entry."*

---

## Scene 3 — Warranties Page (20s)

1. Click **Warranties** in sidebar
2. Show Active tab — Dell XPS (661 days), iPhone (271 days), Sony headphones (313 days)

> *"Every warranty tracked automatically. No more losing receipts and voiding warranties."*

---

## Scene 4 — Ask Vault (30s)

1. Click **Ask Vault**
2. Type: `How much did I spend on electronics?`  
   → Answer: *"Found 3 receipts totalling INR 249,980"*
3. Type: `Which documents do I have for internship onboarding?`  
   → Answer cites degree, PAN, Aadhaar, bank statement, offer letter

> *"Plain English. No search syntax. It knows your vault."*

---

## Scene 5 — Create Package (30s)

1. Switch to **Create Package** tab
2. Type: `internship onboarding`
3. Click **Suggest** → AI selects: Degree, Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Statement, Offer Letter
4. Deselect one to show manual control
5. Click **Review & Create Share Link**

> *"AI suggests. You decide. Nothing is shared without your explicit confirmation."*

---

## Scene 6 — Secure Share (20s)

1. In ShareModal: set **24 hours**, max **3 downloads**, label `HR Team`
2. Check confirmation box → **Create Share Link**
3. Copy the link

> *"Time-limited, download-limited, labelled. The recipient sees only these specific files."*

---

## Scene 7 — Revoke (10s)

1. Go to **Shared Links**
2. Click **Revoke** on the share
3. Status instantly → **Revoked**

> *"Revoked. Dead immediately. The link returns 403 if anyone tries to use it."*
